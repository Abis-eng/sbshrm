# Gmail Email Setup Instructions

## Step 1: Enable 2-Step Verification (Required for App Passwords)

1. Go to: https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Sign in with: **technologiessbs15@gmail.com**
3. Scroll down to "How you sign in to Google"
4. Click on **"2-Step Verification"**
5. Click **"Get Started"**
6. Follow the steps to enable 2-Step Verification:
   - Enter your password
   - Add your phone number
   - Verify with a code sent to your phone
   - Click "Turn On"

## Step 2: Generate App Password

After 2-Step Verification is enabled:

1. Go back to: https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Scroll down to "2-Step Verification" section
3. Click on **"App Passwords"** (this will now be visible)
4. You may need to sign in again
5. Select:
   - **App:** Mail
   - **Device:** Other (Custom name)
   - **Name:** HRM System
6. Click **"Generate"**
7. Copy the 16-character password (it will look like: `abcd efgh ijkl mnop`)
8. **Important:** Copy it immediately - you won't see it again!

## Step 3: Use App Password in HRM System

1. Go to Settings → Company Settings
2. Scroll to "Email Configuration"
3. Enter the 16-character App Password in "Email Password" field (remove spaces if any)
4. Make sure "Enable Email" is checked
5. Click "Save Settings"
6. Test by sending a test email

## Alternative: If You Can't Enable 2-Step Verification

If you're using a Google Workspace account or can't enable 2-Step Verification:

1. Check with your Google Workspace admin
2. Or try using your regular password (may not work if "Less secure app access" is disabled)
3. Contact Google Support for assistance

## Troubleshooting

- **"App Passwords" option not showing:** Make sure 2-Step Verification is fully enabled
- **Password not working:** Make sure you copied the entire 16-character password correctly
- **Still can't connect:** Check firewall/network settings, port 587 should be open
